# Contributing to Loquia

Contributions are welcome. By submitting a pull request, you confirm that you
have the right to contribute the material and license that contribution under
Apache-2.0.

Please:

- keep changes focused and explain their purpose;
- preserve copyright, attribution, licence and NOTICE files;
- disclose any third-party code, models or assets and their licences;
- do not use personal data in examples or test fixtures.

Forks and derivative projects are welcome under Apache-2.0. They must not
present themselves as the official Loquia project or imply endorsement by
EMH01. See TRADEMARKS.md.
